Stampport

A stamp for every country you visit

Passport memories, in one book

Entry stamps are the best souvenir of a trip, but pages fill up, passports get renewed and the stamps are gone. Stampport gives you your own stamp book: pick the country you visited, press its stamp anywhere on the page at any angle, and flip the pages with your finger.

Your book

  • Pick a country249 countries and territories, searchable by English or Turkish name or ISO code.
  • Choose a stampRound, rectangle, oval or double frame in five ink colors. Optionally add a city or region and the visit date.
  • Press itDrag, rotate and resize with two fingers, then press. The stamp settles onto the paper like real ink.
  • Flip the pagesAdd pages and turn them with a realistic paper curl.

And more

  • TimelineYour stamps in date order; tap Play to watch your journey being stamped again.
  • Bucket listList the countries you want to visit; they are checked off when you stamp them.
  • Multiple booksA separate book for every trip, with five paper themes.
  • Share a pageShare your page as an image with friends.
  • Back up and syncOptionally create an account to back up your books and sync them across your phones.

Principles

  • A memory book, not proof. You say where you have been; no location permission, no verification.
  • No account required. Without an account everything stays on your phone.
  • No ads, no tracking. No ads, advertising ID, analytics or crash reporting (see the privacy policy).
  • Data in Europe. With an account, your books are stored in Google's European data centers and only you can access them.
  • Delete your account yourself. Right in the app, no email needed.
