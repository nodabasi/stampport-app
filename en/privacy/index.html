Privacy Policy

What we collect, where it lives, how to delete it

Effective: November 6, 2026 · Stampport mobile app for iOS and Android

Summary

If you use Stampport without an account, none of your information reaches us: your books, stamps and settings stay on your phone. Creating an account is optional and is used only to back up your books and sync them across your phones. The app has no ads, advertising ID, analytics or crash reporting, and never asks for your location.

Data controller

The app is published by an independent developer: Mehmet Nusret Odabaşı. Contact: odabasi.developer@gmail.com.

Using the app without an account

Without an account, your books, pages, stamps (country, template, color, position on the page, optional city name and visit date), your bucket list and settings are stored only on your phone. They are never sent to a server and are deleted when you uninstall the app or use Settings → Reset all data.

What we collect if you create an account

  • Account information — your email address and password, or, if you sign in with Google, your Google account's email address and name. Passwords are stored encrypted by Google's Firebase Authentication; neither we nor the app can see them.
  • Book content — your books' names and themes, pages, stamps (country, template, color, position and angle on the page, and the city/region and visit date if you entered them), your bucket list, and when these records changed.

Language, effects and haptics settings are not sent to the cloud.

What we do not collect

  • Location, phone number, contacts or photo library.
  • Advertising ID or ad interactions. The app has no ads.
  • Usage statistics, analytics or crash logs.

How we use it

Only to recognize your account, back up your books and deliver them to the phones you sign in on. Your data is never sold, rented, or used for advertising or profiling.

Where it is stored and who can see it

Account information and book content are stored with Google's Firebase services (Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore). The Firestore database is located in European data centers (eur3). Database rules ensure that each user can read and write only their own records; no other user can see your books. Google processes this data on our behalf solely to provide the service.

When you share a page, the image is created on your phone and goes only to the app you choose.

Retention

For as long as your account exists. When you delete a stamp or book, its cloud record is deleted too; only an empty deletion marker remains so your other phones know it was removed. When you delete your account, all your cloud data and the account are permanently deleted.

Your rights

Under the GDPR and Turkey's KVKK you have the right to access, correct and delete your data and to object to its processing. You can view and edit your books in the app and delete your account from within the app (steps). For other requests, write to odabasi.developer@gmail.com.

Children

Stampport is suitable for all ages and can be used without an account. Creating an account is optional; we recommend that children under 13 do so with a parent's supervision.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page and the date above will be updated.
