Delete Account

Permanently delete your account in the app

You do not need to email us to delete your Stampport account; it takes a few taps in the app.

Steps

  • Open SettingsThe gear icon at the top right of the book screen.
  • Tap Account & backupAt the top of the list.
  • Tap Delete accountBelow the Sign out button.
  • Confirm it is youEnter your password for an email account, or sign in with Google for a Google account.

What is deleted

  • Your account (email address and sign-in details).
  • All your books, pages, stamps and bucket list in the cloud.
  • The books on that phone are deleted too and the app starts with an empty book.

Deletion happens immediately and cannot be undone. If you signed in with Google, the app's access to your Google account is revoked as well.

If you cannot access the app, you can request deletion by writing from your registered email address to odabasi.developer@gmail.com. Requests are completed within 30 days.
