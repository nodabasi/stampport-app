Frequently asked questions

Do I need an account?

No. Stampport works fully without an account; your books stay on your phone. With an account, your books are backed up and synced to any phone you sign in on.

What happens to my stamps if I change phones?

If you have an account, sign in on the new phone and your books come back. Without an account the data lives only on the old phone; we recommend creating one under Settings → Account & backup before switching.

Does the app use my location?

No. Stampport is a memory book; you choose the countries yourself. Location permission is never requested.

I pressed a stamp in the wrong place. How do I fix it?

Tap the stamp in your book. You can then drag it, rotate and resize it with two fingers, add a date or city with the buttons below, or delete it. Tap Done when finished.

Why are country names in Turkish?

The app follows your phone's language. Choose English or Türkçe under Settings → Language; country names on stamps change too.

I signed out. Are my books gone?

No. Your books stay in the cloud and are only removed from this phone. Sign in with the same account and they come back.

I forgot my password.

On Settings → Account & backup, enter your email and tap Forgot password; a reset link will be emailed to you.

How do I delete my account?

Right in the app; see the account deletion page.
